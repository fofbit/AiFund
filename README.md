## The Problem

The global financial system is inherently unequal. Sophisticated trading algorithms, real-time market intelligence, and diversified portfolio strategies are accessible only to those who can afford Bloomberg terminals, hedge fund minimums, or years of financial education.

**7.7 billion people are locked out.**

## The Solution

AiFund deploys a personal AI trading Bot for every user — starting from just **$1**. Your Bot monitors global markets 24/7, identifies opportunities across asset classes, and delivers actionable intelligence directly to you.

You don't need a bank account. You don't need a degree in finance. You don't need connections. You need $1 and a crypto wallet.

## How It Works

```
Connect Wallet → Activate ($1) → Adopt Your Bot → Follow Signals → Grow Wealth
```

1. **Connect** — Link any crypto wallet (MetaMask, OKX, Phantom, Unisat, etc.)
2. **Activate** — Deposit as little as $1 in any supported cryptocurrency
3. **Adopt** — Your personal AI Bot starts analyzing global markets immediately
4. **Follow** — Receive daily buy/sell signals across crypto, stocks, futures, and more
5. **Grow** — VIP members connect exchange APIs for fully automated 24/7 trading

## VIP System

Every VIP starts at Level 1 with $100 maximum managed capital. Each profit-sharing cycle increases the level by 1. After 100 levels, Bot manages up to $10,000.

**The 50/50 Rule:** 50% of earnings compound in the account. 50% must be withdrawn to improve your actual life. We believe wealth should change lives, not just grow numbers.

**Platform fee:** 10% of profits. Zero when there is no profit.
